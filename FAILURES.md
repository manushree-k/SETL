# Failures

What broke while building Setl, and how it got fixed. Updated as it happens, not reconstructed at the end.

| Date | What broke | Root cause | Fix | Time lost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-30 | Pass 3 could silently link a bank credit direction (a) had already refused as ambiguous. Scenario: two ₹500 settlements, one unattributed ₹500 bank credit. Direction (a) correctly found 2 subsets and refused. Direction (b) then ran per-settlement and, considering the two settlements one at a time, saw the *same* still-unclaimed bank credit as the only candidate for whichever settlement it checked first — called that "unique," and linked it. | `lib/engine/pass3-aggregate.ts`: direction (b)'s bank-line pool filter only excluded `claimedBankLineNos`, not bank lines direction (a) had already flagged ambiguous — so an ambiguous credit looked freshly available once considered from the other side. Found via an integration smoke test in `run.ts` (prompt 09c), not by testing pass3 in isolation. | Track `ambiguousBankLineNos` from direction (a)'s refusals; exclude them from direction (b)'s candidate pool too. Verified against 3 cases: the blueprint's own split example, the mirror-direction case, and the bug scenario isolated (all correct, no regressions). | ~20 min |
| 2026-08-30 | Live-DB run of `main` batch: only 2 of 15 settlements reconciled to the bank (expected: majority). | `lib/engine/pass1-utr.ts`: the duplicate-credit check flagged *any* second bank line sharing a UTR as `DUPLICATE_CREDIT`, regardless of amount — conflating injected case 7 (true duplicate: same UTR, same amount) with case 5 (split payout: "same UTR prefix but different amounts"). A genuine split-payout's second bank line got dropped from matching entirely before ever reaching Pass 3's subset-sum. Confirmed directly: bank lines 3 & 4 (same UTR `1009526630jasblf`, amounts 1982811 + 2310846 = 4293657, exactly `setl_b02381c76b604b`'s amount) were being wrongly excluded. | Track `seenByUtrAndAmount` (UTR → amount → first bank line); only flag `DUPLICATE_CREDIT` when *both* UTR and amount match an earlier line. Different amount → not a duplicate, both lines flow through to Pass 2/3 normally. Verified: true duplicate (lines 1/2, same UTR+amount) still flags correctly; split payout (lines 3/4, same UTR, different amounts) now reaches Pass 3 and links. Bank-to-settlement links went 2→4 on live re-run. | ~45 min |
| 2026-08-30 | After the Pass 1 fix, still only 2-4/15 settlements reconciled — nowhere near "majority." | `scripts/generate.ts`'s `injectAggregatedCredit` paired settlements to merge into one bank credit by taking consecutive entries from a random shuffle (`available[i]`/`available[i+1]`), with zero check on date proximity. Confirmed: `setl_0a225c5ab2acd3` (2026-03-19) and `setl_d62597b893494b` (2026-03-10) — 9 days apart — got merged into one bank line (`14832917 = 4702159 + 10130758`, exact). The math was genuine, but Pass 3's own ±2-day candidate-pool window (SETL_BLUEPRINT.md section 10 — the engine's own literal spec, not something loosened to find this) can never see a pair that far apart. | Rewrote the pairing loop: for each candidate `a`, scan the rest of the shuffled pool for the first still-unused settlement within `daysBetweenIST(a, candidate) <= 2` (and with a real bank line via `findBankLineForSettlement`). If none found in the current pool, skip that injection attempt rather than force a distant pair. Verified: both pairs still fire (full budget, 2/2), both now correctly link via Pass 3 and classify `PARTIAL_SETTLEMENT`/`AUTO_RESOLVED` at confidence 0.9. All 14 injected-case counts unchanged after regeneration; CSVs byte-identical across two runs of the same seed (determinism intact). | ~1 hr |
| 2026-08-30 | Same investigation: a genuine split-payout pair (bank lines 7/8, same UTR `9763647321oloivd`) summed to `8522536`, but their settlement (`setl_4dab63f2969f35`) showed header amount `8520674` — an ₹18.62 gap, too large for rounding, too precise for coincidence. Landed in the same commit as 09B, `e5448d5` (not its own commit — flagged to the user at the time). | `scripts/generate.ts`'s `injectFeeOvercharge` (via `applyFeeOverride`) and `injectAmbiguousMatch` (via `applyAmountOverride`) both mutate a settlement's header amount, tracked only through `claimedLineIds` — a set completely decoupled from `claimedSettlementIds`, which the bank-line-level injectors (split/aggregate/duplicate/corrupted-narration) use. Either could pick a line inside a settlement a bank-line injector had *already* restructured, silently stacking a fee/amount change on top of a bank line already built from the pre-mutation amount. | Both functions now also take `claimedSettlementIds` and exclude any line whose `settlement_id` is already claimed, before mutating. Verified via regeneration: all 14 injected-case counts still match budget exactly (no starvation), `tsc`/tests clean. | ~30 min |
| 2026-08-30 | **FIXED.** `settlement_composition`'s `UNMATCHED_TO_BANK` count was 8/15 on the regenerated `main` batch, most of it *not* explained by any of the four bugs above. | `scripts/generate.ts`'s `injectTimingDifference` and `injectPartialSettlement` move payment lines between settlements (`addLineToSettlement`/`removeLineFromSettlement`), changing both settlements' header amounts — tracked *only* via `claimedLineIds`, never `claimedSettlementIds`. `generateBatch`'s pipeline ran `generateBankLines` once, **before** `injectAnomalies` did anything at all. So every settlement either of these two touched (source or destination) permanently lost its bank match, *unless* a later bank-line-rebuilding injector (split/aggregate/duplicate/corrupted-narration — all of which correctly reconstruct a bank line from the *current*, already-mutated `settlement.amount_paise` via `findBankLineForSettlement`) happened to also pick that same settlement. Confirmed directly: `setl_d6d23fcc538619` received two lines moved in by `injectTimingDifference`; no bank line in `data/main/bank_statement.csv` came within ₹2 of its current amount. Before fixing, checked whether any bank-line-level injector actually *reads* `batch.bankLines` before it exists (it doesn't matter which — none of `injectSplitPayout`/`injectAggregatedCredit`/`injectDuplicateCredit`/`injectMissingInBank`/`injectCorruptedNarration` care about anything except the *current* settlement amount at the moment they run) — that's what made a pipeline reorder safe rather than requiring five more places to change. | Reordered `generateBatch`/`injectAnomalies`: `injectFeeOvercharge` and `injectAmbiguousMatch` moved up to run alongside `injectTimingDifference`/`injectPartialSettlement` (all four are settlement-amount-mutating, line-level, and touch `batch.bankLines` never), then `generateBankLines` runs exactly once — after every settlement amount is final — then the five bank-line-reading injectors run as before, then `injectRoundingResidual` last (unchanged position: its whole point is an *intentional* small mismatch against an already-built bank line). No changes needed inside any of the five bank-line-reading injectors themselves. Verified: determinism intact (byte-identical CSVs, same seed), all 14 injected-case counts unchanged, `tsc`/tests clean, conservation identities A/B held with zero throws. **`FULLY_RECONCILED` 3/15 → 10/15, `UNMATCHED_TO_BANK` 8/15 → 1/15** (the one remaining is the genuine `missing_in_bank` case, by design), bank-to-settlement links 9→16. Committed `8aaa3e7`. | ~1.5 hrs (diagnosis + fix + regeneration + full re-verification) |
| 2026-08-30 | **OPEN — not fixed.** `settlement_composition` shows `status: DISCREPANCY`, `discrepancy_component: BANK_CREDIT` for all 4 settlements in the two `PARTIAL_SETTLEMENT`/aggregated-credit pairs, even though both pairs are correctly classified `PARTIAL_SETTLEMENT`/`AUTO_RESOLVED` at confidence 0.9 in `exceptions` — the surface that actually matters is right; only the composition drill-down is misleading. | `lib/engine/pass6b-compose.ts`'s `bank_credit_total` sums the *full* credit of every bank line linked to a settlement — correct when one bank line maps to one settlement, but when Pass 3 links ONE shared bank credit to TWO settlements (direction a: many-settlements-one-credit), each settlement's composition independently shows the *entire* shared credit as its own `bank_credit_total`, not its proportional share. E.g. `setl_5570af976045db` (header 8606258) and `setl_446441e559f1b9` (header 5759989) both show `bank_credit_total: 14366247` (their sum) — so each settlement's `diff_total` comes out equal to *minus the other settlement's amount*, landing on `DISCREPANCY`/`BANK_CREDIT` instead of some status that reflects "this is a correctly-resolved shared credit." This is a pre-existing gap in `pass6b-compose.ts` as originally written (09B), not something today's generator fixes introduced — it just never got exercised against real data with a genuine, correctly-linked shared credit until the Pass 1 / aggregated-credit / pipeline-reorder fixes made that case reachable. | Not yet decided. The blueprint's own Pass 6B formula (section 10) literally says `bank_credit_total = Σ credit on bank lines linked to this settlement` with no proration step specified, so this may need a genuinely new design decision (how to split a shared credit's diff/status across the settlements it funds), not just a bugfix to existing logic. `lib/engine/pass6b-compose.ts` and possibly `lib/engine/run.ts`'s composition-evidence wiring would be in scope. | not started |
